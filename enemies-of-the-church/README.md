# Enemies of the Church

A collection of studies on the movements, ideologies, and secret societies that the Catholic Church has identified as opposed to her doctrine, discipline, and mission — and the Church's magisterial response to them across the centuries.

These documents are written to scholarly standards, grounded in primary sources — papal bulls and encyclicals, declarations of the dicasteries of the Holy See, conciliar decrees, and the Catechism of the Catholic Church. The Church distinguishes clearly between the **doctrinal condemnation** of error (which is her perennial duty) and the **violent persecution** of persons (which she has always rejected); these documents maintain that distinction rigorously.
