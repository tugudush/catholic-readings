# Resurrection of the Lord Parish, Iligan City

Resurrection of the Lord Parish is located at Lanao Chung Hua Road, Iligan City, Lanao del Norte, in the Diocese of Iligan (Vicariate of St. Michael). Some directory listings append "Filipino-Chinese Church" to the parish name, reflecting its service to Iligan's Filipino-Chinese Catholic community. The timetable below was published by Philmass and represents the weekly schedule displayed for 27 September through 3 October 2026.
