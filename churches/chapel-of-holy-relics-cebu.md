# Chapel of the Holy Relics, OAD Middle Tabor Hill, Cebu City

The Chapel of the Holy Relics at OAD Middle Tabor Hill publishes a Sunday Latin Mass schedule. The timetable below reflects the information available online when checked on 2 October 2026; contact the chapel before travelling because schedules may change, particularly on solemnities and during special events.
