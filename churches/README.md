# Catholic Church Schedules

This directory records local parish and cathedral information, including published Mass timetables, confession times, contact details, and links for verification. These schedules are pastoral reference data and should be confirmed with the church before travel, especially on solemnities, holidays, and during parish events.

## Source Convention

The Iligan parish schedules in these records were transcribed from [Mass Schedules Philippines](https://www.mass-schedules.com/), which cautions that its data may not be current; the timetable for Resurrection of the Lord Parish is taken from [Philmass](https://www.philmass.com/) because the Mass Schedules Philippines page for that parish publishes no timetable. The Cebu Latin Mass schedule is taken from the [OAD Asia Holy Mass Schedule](https://www.oadasia.org/holy-mass-schedule) and cross-referenced with a Philippine diocesan TLM directory. Each record identifies its sources and when the information was checked where available. Confirm schedules directly with the church before travelling.
