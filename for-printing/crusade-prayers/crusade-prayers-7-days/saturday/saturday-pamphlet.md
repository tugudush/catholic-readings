# Saturday — Crusade of Prayers

**Theme:** Prayers to God the Father.
**Time:** about 35 minutes for the litanies and Crusade Prayers; about 65 minutes with the Holy Rosary and the Divine Mercy Chaplet.

## 1. The Order of Saturday's Prayers

1. Begin with the daily prayers, the Litany Prayers (1)–(6), and the daily Crusade Prayers, as printed in _Daily Crusade Prayers — A Pocket Pamphlet_.
2. Say the Crusade Prayers for Saturday, in the order printed in section 2.
3. Say the Holy Rosary, the Joyful Mysteries, and the Divine Mercy Chaplet, as set out in section 3.
4. Close with Crusade Prayer (96) and the Sign of the Cross, as set out in section 4.

## 2. Crusade Prayers for Saturday

**Crusade of Prayer (5): Praise to God the Most High.**

    O, Eternal Father, we offer you our prayers in joyful thanksgiving
    for your precious gift of Mercy to the whole of mankind.
    We rejoice and offer you most glorious King our praise and adoration
    for your loving and tender mercy.
    You, God the Most High are our King,
    and for this gift you now bring to us we lie at your feet in humble servitude.
    Please God have mercy on all your children. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (19): Prayer for young people.**

    Mother of Salvation, I ask you to pray for the mercy of young souls,
    who are in terrible darkness,
    so that they recognize your Beloved Son when He comes to redeem the whole of mankind.
    Let not one soul fall by the wayside.
    Let not one soul reject His Great Mercy.
    I pray, Mother, that all are saved and ask you to cover these souls
    with your Holy Mantle to provide them the protection they need from the deceiver. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (20): Stop the antichrist from destroying the world.**

    O, God the Father, in the name of your precious Son,
    I call on you to prevent the Anti-Christ from ensnaring the souls of your children.
    I beg you, Almighty Father, to stop him from inflicting terror on your children.
    I beg you to stop him from contaminating your creation
    and ask you to have mercy on those poor souls who will be powerless against him.
    Hear my prayer, dear Father, and save all your children from this terrible evil. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (25): For Protection of visionaries around the world.**

    O, God of the Most High, I plead with you to offer protection to all your holy messengers in the world.
    I pray that they are protected from the hatred of others.
    I ask that your most Holy Word is spread quickly all over the world.
    Protect your messengers from slander, abuse, lies and every kind of danger.
    Protect their families and cover them with the Holy Spirit at all times
    so that the messages they give to the world are heeded with contrite and humble hearts. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (26): Pray the Rosary to help save your nation.**

Say the Holy Rosary, the Joyful Mysteries, for your nation, as set out in section 3.

**Crusade of Prayer (27): Prayer for Peace in the World.**

    O, My Jesus, I beg for mercy for those afflicted by terrible wars.
    I plead for peace to be instilled in those tortured nations who are blind to the truth of your existence.
    Please cover these nations with the power of the Holy Spirit
    so that they will stop their pursuit of power over innocent souls.
    Have mercy on all your countries who are powerless against the evil atrocities which cover the whole world. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (42): Prayer of fasting to stop One World Currency.**

    O God Most High, I offer you my gift of fasting
    so that you will stop the grip of evil in the world being planned to starve my country of food including the bread of life.
    Accept my offering and listen to my pleas for other nations
    to prevent them from the suffering being planned by the anti-christ.
    Save us Dear Lord from this wickedness and protect our faith
    so that we can honor you with the freedom we need to love and adore you forever and ever. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (52): Prayer to the Father.**

    My dearest Father, in the name of your precious Son and in remembrance of His Passion on the Cross,
    I call out to you You, God the Most High, Creator of the World and all that is hold our salvation in your Holy Hands,
    embrace all of your children, including those who don't know You and those who do but look the other way.
    Forgive us our sin and save us from the persecution of satan and his army.
    Take us into your arms and fill us with the hope. We need to see the way of the truth. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (54): Prayer to the Father to dilute impact of World War 3.**

    O Heavenly Father in the name of your Beloved Son, Jesus Christ who suffered greatly for the sins of mankind
    please help us in these difficult times we face.
    Help us to survive the persecution being planned by greedy rulers and those who want to destroy your Churches and your children.
    We implore you Dear Father to help feed our families and save the lives of those who will be forced into a war against their will.
    We love you Dear Father, we beg you to help us in our time of need. Save us from the grip of the antichrist.
    Help us to survive his mark, the mark of the beast, by refusing to accept it.
    Help those who love you to remain true to Your Holy Word at all times
    so that You can bestow on us the graces to survive in Body and Soul. Amen.

**Crusade of Prayer (59): A Pledge of Allegiance to the Divine Will.**

    O God the Most High, O Heavenly Father,
    I pledge to You my firm allegiance to honor and obey You in all things united to Your Divine Will on earth.
    I, through the Sacred Blood of Your only beloved Son, the True Messiah,
    offer You my mind, my body and my soul on behalf of all souls
    so that we can unite as one in Your Heavenly Kingdom to come

**Crusade of Prayer (61): Avert One World Control.**

## 3. The Holy Rosary and the Divine Mercy Chaplet

**The Holy Rosary: the Joyful Mysteries.** Say the Joyful Mysteries on Saturday. Each decade begins with one Our Father, ten Hail Marys and one Glory Be, while meditating on:

1. The Annunciation: the angel Gabriel greets Mary (Luke 1:26–38).
2. The Visitation: Mary visits Elizabeth (Luke 1:39–56).
3. The Nativity: the birth of Jesus at Bethlehem (Luke 2:1–20).
4. The Presentation: Jesus is presented in the Temple (Luke 2:22–38).
5. The Finding in the Temple: Jesus, aged twelve, among the teachers (Luke 2:41–50).

**Divine Mercy Chaplet.** Said daily, as set out in _How to Pray the Chaplet of Divine Mercy_.

## 5. A Note on These Texts

These are Crusade Prayers from _The Book of Truth_ (Maria Divine Mercy), an **unapproved** body of alleged private revelation. Several bishops have prohibited it or judged it contrary to Catholic teaching. It is printed for study and private devotion, not for belief, and it is not Church teaching or an approved liturgical text.

**What the prayers ask.** The petitions addressed to God, Our Lady and the saints, for mercy, for peace, for the conversion of sinners, for the protection of the Church and of the weak, and for the gift of faith, are in themselves in harmony with Catholic prayer. The Church asks for the same graces in the Mass and in the Holy Rosary.

**What the prayers claim.** The "one world currency" and "one world control" (prayers 42 and 61), the coming "World War 3" (prayer 54), the Antichrist and the mark of the beast, and the "Warning" are assertions of the corpus, not of the Church. They must not be treated as fulfilled or imminent events. Catholic teaching does not ask the faithful to fear predicted events. It asks for vigilance and hope: "Watch therefore, for you do not know on which day your Lord is coming" (Matthew 24:42).

**Private revelation.** The public Revelation of Jesus Christ is complete. Private revelations do not add to it or correct it, and no one is obliged to believe them (CCC 66–67). A text printed here carries only the authority of the prayer itself, judged by its agreement with the faith.

**Dates and feasts.** Crusade Prayer (154) names 4 June as a Feast Day of the Mother of Salvation. No such feast is in the Church's liturgical calendar, and the date should not be presented as one.

**Order of prayer.** No prayer in this pamphlet replaces the Mass, the Sacraments, or the public prayers of the Church, which remain the foundation of Catholic devotion. The Holy Rosary is the Church's own prayer for the same intentions, and the Divine Mercy Chaplet is a devotion the Church has commended.

**Discernment.** Catholics are asked to "test everything; hold fast what is good" (1 Thessalonians 5:21). A private message is judged by its agreement with Sacred Scripture, Tradition and the teaching of the Church, and by its fruits. Messages that stir fear, division or distrust of the Church's pastors, or that fix a date for themselves, call for particular caution.

**Pastoral counsel.** Those who pray these texts in a group should keep the Mass and the Sacrament of Penance at the centre of their parish life, and should seek guidance on the texts from their parish priest or their diocesan bishop. The leader should read the texts beforehand, so that each prayer is said with understanding and never as a condition for grace or a test of faith.
