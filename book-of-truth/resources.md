# The Book of Truth — Resources: Where the Primary Text Circulates

> ⚠️ **Not approved by the Church.** This page is a pointer list, not an endorsement. It records **where the corpus's own text can be obtained for study**. Several bishops and dioceses have prohibited the corpus or judged it contrary to Catholic faith; nothing here is proposed for belief. For the Church's judgment, see [church-assessment-and-warnings.md](church-assessment-and-warnings.md); for how to read it rightly, see [catholic-discernment-guide.md](catholic-discernment-guide.md).

---

## 1. Purpose and Caveats

The original promoters' website, **thewarningsecondcoming.com, is defunct**, and the corpus now circulates through mirrors, PDF compilations, print-on-demand books, and archive sites. This page gathers those sources so that a reader studying the corpus can find the primary text.

Three cautions apply to everything below:

1. **These are not Church sources.** Nearly every free mirror is run by the promoters themselves or by third parties sympathetic to the corpus; they present it favourably and without doctrinal caveats.
2. **Editions differ.** Page numbering, ordering, and whether prayers are included vary between the printed volumes, the PDF compilations, and the online archives. Always cite the specific edition you actually used.
3. **Links rot.** The corpus's distribution is decentralised and sites appear and disappear; where a link is dead, try the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. All URLs below were checked on **2 October 2026**.

## 7. Reliability and Citation Notes

- **Cite the edition.** Because the free PDFs, the online archive, and the printed volumes order and number the material differently, a citation should name the source (e.g. "Mary Refuge of Souls PDF, 4 March 2015 edition") rather than a bare message number.
- **The free mirrors are promoter-run or unauthorised third-party uploads**, not the publisher; they are convenient but not authoritative even about the corpus itself.
- **The messages are also circulated in many languages.** The Mary Refuge of Souls pages and the promoters' sites host translated editions; translations vary in quality.
- **Nothing here has any ecclesiastical approval**, and several local ordinaries have forbidden dissemination in their jurisdictions. Use these sources for study only.
