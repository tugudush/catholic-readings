# Fr. Chris Alar on the End Times: The Five Biblical Signs and the Triumph of the Immaculate Heart

_A structured theological synthesis of an interview with Fr. Chris Alar, MIC, on the program "Historian Catoliko" (hosted by Adrian), published August 2025. Fr. Alar is a priest of the Marian Fathers of the Immaculate Conception and the host of the "Explaining the Faith" series on the Divine Mercy YouTube channel. This synthesis presents his catechesis on the Catholic end-times timeline — the five biblical signs of the Second Coming, the order of events according to the saints, the warnings of Fatima and Akita, the three days of darkness, the Antichrist, and the primacy of the sacraments — while carefully distinguishing dogma from private revelation and theological opinion._

## 1. The Five Biblical Signs of the Second Coming

Fr. Alar opens his teaching by grounding the discussion not in private revelation but in Sacred Scripture itself — specifically, five signs that must precede the Second Coming of Christ (the _Parousia_, from the Greek παρουσία, "presence" or "divine manifestation").

The Catechism of the Catholic Church (CCC 670) teaches that "since the Ascension, God's plan has entered into its fulfillment. We are already at 'the last hour.' Already the final stage of the world is with us, and the renewal of the world is underway." Fr. Alar cites this passage to frame the entire discussion: the Church has been living in the end times since the apostolic age, and the signs enumerated in Scripture mark the progressive unfolding of this final stage.

### Sign 1: The Gospel Preached to the Whole World

> "And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached throughout the whole world, as a testimony to all nations; and then the end will come." — [Matthew 24:14](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=mt&g=24&p=14)

Fr. Alar observes that with the advent of the internet, livestreaming, and global communications infrastructure, the penetration of the Gospel into all nations has reached an unprecedented scope. He cites the example of the Marian Fathers' own live-streaming apostolate during the COVID-19 pandemic, which extended their reach globally. Even in developing nations, he notes, widespread access to mobile phones means that Christ's message reaches populations historically difficult to evangelize.

### Sign 2: The Great Apostasy

> "Let no one deceive you in any way; for that day will not come, unless the rebellion comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of perdition." — [2 Thessalonians 2:3](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=2th&g=2&p=3)

The _apostasia_ — the great falling away from the faith — is described by Fr. Alar as a massive departure from Catholicism marked by heresy, false teachings, and false religions. He identifies several manifestations already visible:

- **Prosperity gospel** preaching that purports to offer material wealth as a sign of divine favor, which he calls "not from the Bible"
- **False teaching within the Church itself**, including "misleading bishops" and priests who teach that homosexual acts are not contrary to Scripture
- **The proliferation of false religions**: Fr. Alar states bluntly that "anything but the Catholic Church is a false religion," echoing the traditional Catholic claim that the fullness of saving truth subsists in the Catholic Church (cf. [_Lumen Gentium_ 8](https://www.magisterium.com/docs/02e749f5-31e3-46ad-b126-97602dd0d8e2/ref/8), CCC 816–819)

Jesus' own warning in [Matthew 24:4–5](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=mt&g=24&p=4) is cited: "See that no one leads you astray. For many will come in my name, saying, 'I am the Christ,' and they will lead many astray."

### Sign 3: The Universal Conversion of the Jews

> "A hardening has come upon part of Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles come in, and so all Israel will be saved." — [Romans 11:25–26](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=rom&g=11&p=25)

Fr. Alar emphasizes that this sign, attested in both St. Paul and the Catechism (CCC 674), has **not yet occurred** — and this is why Pope Benedict XVI said we are not yet at the end. St. Thomas Aquinas, in his commentary on Romans, affirmed that "all Jews will come into the Church" before the end.

He draws a careful distinction between **Zionism** (a secular political movement) and the **chosen people of God** referenced in Scripture. The importance of Israel in the eschatological drama, he insists, lies not in geopolitical alignments but in the prophecy that "the world can't end until they accept Jesus."

The Catechism echoes this: "The glorious Messiah's coming is suspended at every moment of history until his recognition by 'all Israel'" (CCC 674). St. Peter's preaching in Acts 3:19–21 is cited: Christ "must remain in heaven until the time for establishing all that God spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets."

Fr. Alar links this to Mariology: "Our Lady, she's Jewish, and she can be the one that brings them back — but first we got to convert our Gentile brothers and sisters."

### Sign 4: The Revelation of the Antichrist

> "Children, it is the last hour; and as you have heard that antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have come." — [1 John 2:18](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=1jn&g=2&p=18)

The full treatment of the Antichrist is covered in [Section 4](#4-the-antichrist-the-son-of-perdition) below. Here Fr. Alar notes that most theologians and Church Fathers hold that the Antichrist will appear three and a half years before the end of the world — a timeline derived from the 42 months mentioned in [Daniel 7:25](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=dan&g=7&p=25) and [Revelation 13:5](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=rev&g=13&p=5). The Book of Revelation, he notes, describes a seven-year preparation for the end of the world, with the Antichrist revealed at the midpoint.

### Sign 5: The Tribulation

> "Then they will deliver you up to tribulation, and put you to death; and you will be hated by all nations for my name's sake." — [Matthew 24:9](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=mt&g=24&p=9)

The tribulation encompasses both natural and man-made calamities: famines, earthquakes, wars, and persecutions. Fr. Alar notes that "we see Christians being slaughtered in Nigeria" and that the present age has produced "more martyrs than any time since the first century."

CCC 675 teaches that "before Christ's second coming, the Church must pass through a final trial that will shake the faith of many believers." Fr. Alar paraphrases this and emphasizes that the persecution will take the form of "religious deception offering men solutions to their problems that are not of God."

Matthew 24–25 is identified as the "Little Apocalypse," foretelling that believers "will be delivered into tribulation" and "hated." Yet Fr. Alar stresses hope: "Christ will tell us what to say. He'll keep us informed. He'll pour out grace like no other time in history."

One helpful corrective he offers: the Greek word ἀποκάλυψις (_apokalypsis_) does not mean "death and destruction" but **"unveiling"** — the unveiling of the past and of heaven.

## 2. The Order of End-Time Events According to the Saints

Moving from Scripture to the consensus of the saints and approved mystics, Fr. Alar presents a synthesized order of eschatological events. He is careful to distinguish this from dogma:

> "This is not dogmatic. The Church does not teach this is something you have to believe. It's just something that I think is worth looking at."

The sequence, as drawn from his study of the saints, the mystics, and the Church Fathers:

1. **Chastisement** — This may take the form of war, famine, plague, or other calamities. It is presented as a divine permission meant to awaken mankind from sin.

2. **The Warning (Illumination of Conscience)** — A supernatural event in which every soul on earth will see itself as God sees it. This is treated in detail in [Section 3](#3-the-warning-illumination-of-conscience).

3. **The Revelation of the Antichrist** — The public manifestation of the "son of perdition," a fully human figure possessed by the devil, who will deceive many. (See [Section 4](#4-the-antichrist-the-son-of-perdition).)

4. **The Three Days of Darkness** — A final, merciful warning: a supernatural darkness over the whole earth lasting three days and three nights. (See [Section 5](#5-the-three-days-of-darkness) and the dedicated treatment at [three-days-of-darkness.md](three-days-of-darkness.md).)

5. **The Era of Peace (Triumph of the Immaculate Heart)** — The fulfillment of Our Lady of Fatima's promise: "In the end, my Immaculate Heart will triumph." (See [Section 6](#6-the-era-of-peace-and-the-triumph-of-the-immaculate-heart).)

6. **The Second Coming of Jesus Christ (Parousia)** — The definitive, glorious return of the Lord in majesty to judge the living and the dead.

Fr. Alar emphasizes the pastoral point: "Jesus is not going to come tomorrow, if you believe prophecy, because we haven't yet seen the triumph of the Immaculate Heart. So there's still time. There is time to repent. So let us have hope."

## 3. The Warning: Illumination of Conscience

The "Warning" or "Illumination of Conscience" is presented by Fr. Alar as a pivotal eschatological event attested by numerous mystics and private revelations. It is defined as a divine intervention in which every soul on earth will see itself as God sees it — the full, unmediated experience of one's own moral state before the divine judgment.

### Scriptural Precedent

Fr. Alar connects this to the "great and terrible day of the Lord" and the concept of judgment beginning with the household of God (cf. [1 Peter 4:17](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=1pe&g=4&p=17)). He cites [Acts 2:20](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=act&g=2&p=20): "The sun shall be turned to darkness and the moon to blood, before the day of the Lord comes, the great and manifest day."

### St. Faustina's Personal Illumination (Diary 36)

Fr. Alar draws on St. Faustina Kowalska's own recorded experience as a type and foreshadowing of this event. In Diary 36, St. Faustina writes that she was "summoned to the judgment seat of God" and "saw the condition of my own soul as God sees it." She states: "I could clearly see all that was displeasing to God. I did not know that even the smallest transgressions will have to be accounted for."

This personal illumination of conscience, Fr. Alar explains, is what the mystics describe as occurring for every soul on earth in the great Warning.

### The Book _The Warning_

Fr. Alar references the book _The Warning_ by Christine Watkins (whom he describes as "a good friend") as a reliable popular treatment of this prophecy. He emphasizes that this is "a very real prophecy from many, many blessings" — meaning it enjoys attestation across multiple approved mystics and private revelations.

### The Aftermath

Fr. Alar notes that according to the prophecy, "up to a third will die of fright when they see their soul" — unable to accept the stark reality of their moral condition. But he also emphasizes that the Warning is fundamentally **an act of mercy**, not condemnation: it is given so that souls might repent before the more severe chastisements that follow.

## 4. The Antichrist: The Son of Perdition

### Biblical Foundation

The figure of the Antichrist appears in multiple New Testament texts:

- [2 Thessalonians 2:3–4](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=2th&g=2&p=3): "the man of lawlessness," "the son of perdition," who "opposes and exalts himself against every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God."
- [1 John 2:18](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=1jn&g=2&p=18): "you have heard that antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have come"
- The Beasts of [Revelation 13](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=rev&g=13): the beast from the sea and the beast from the land
- The "great red dragon" of [Revelation 12](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=rev&g=12)

### Nature and Characteristics

Fr. Alar enumerates several characteristics drawn from the consensus of the Church Fathers and approved theologians:

1. **Fully human, not the devil**: He insists on the Thomistic principle that only God has the power of incarnation; the devil cannot become incarnate. The Antichrist will be a fully human person, though "possessed by the devil."

2. **Jewish origin**: Some theological traditions hold that he will be of Jewish lineage, though this is a theological opinion, not a defined teaching.

3. **Persuasive and powerful**: He will "work deceptive miracles" and "deceive the people." Fr. Alar notes that figures like Nero, Caligula, Stalin, Hitler, Mao Zedong, and Lenin "persecuted the righteous and they were bad — but this Antichrist will be worse."

4. **The leader of the apostasy**: He will be "the leader of that apostasy, running people away" from the faith.

5. **Deep hatred for the saints**: He will possess a particular enmity toward the Church and the faithful.

6. **Self-glorification**: CCC 675 is cited: "The Antichrist glorifying man, glorifying himself in place of God — he'll be like the Messiah coming in the flesh."

### The Red Dragon: Communist China

Fr. Alar offers a **personal theological opinion** (not a Church teaching) that the "red dragon" of Revelation 12 may represent communist China. He qualifies this carefully: "The Chinese people are wonderful. The government is evil." This opinion echoes a broader current in certain Catholic circles that see totalitarian communist regimes, particularly the Chinese Communist Party's persecution of Christians (including the underground Catholic Church loyal to Rome), as a type or foreshadowing of the eschatological persecution.

### Timing

Most Church Fathers and theologians, Fr. Alar notes, hold that the Antichrist will appear three and a half years before the end of the world — the 42 months of [Daniel 7:25](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=dan&g=7&p=25) and [Revelation 13:5](https://www.drbo.org/x/d?b=drb&c=rev&g=13&p=5) — at the midpoint of a seven-year period of preparation.

### The Divine Protection

Fr. Alar offers the consoling note that "we will be given special graces at this time." He emphasizes the importance of the Sacrament of Confirmation — "make sure that you're confirmed" — as a sacramental strengthening for the final trial.

## 5. The Three Days of Darkness

> _For a comprehensive treatment of this devotion, including the full range of mystics, the 1915 Holy Office decree, and the 2024 Dicastery norms, see the dedicated document:_ [_The Three Days of Darkness_](three-days-of-darkness.md).

Fr. Alar presents the three days of darkness as the **penultimate event** before the era of peace — a final, merciful warning that is "a mercy to stop us from suffering even more" after the persecution under the Antichrist.

### Biblical Precedent

He identifies two biblical foreshadowings:

- **Exodus 10:22–23**: The ninth plague of Egypt, in which "there was thick darkness over all the land of Egypt for three days," yet "all the people of Israel had light where they dwelt." This typological parallel is significant: just as the Israelites were protected during the Egyptian darkness, so the faithful will be protected in the eschatological darkness.

- **Acts 2:20**: "The sun shall be turned to darkness and the moon to blood, before the day of the Lord comes, the great and manifest day."

### St. Faustina's Testimony (Diary 83)

Fr. Alar cites St. Faustina's Diary 83, in which she records that "all the lights of heaven will be extinguished and there will be like this darkness over the whole earth." She further records that "the sign of the cross will be seen in the sky, and from the openings where the hands and the feet of Jesus were — where He was nailed — great lights will light up the earth." St. Faustina states that "this will take place shortly before the last day."

### Blessed Elizabeth Canori Mora (1820)

Blessed Elizabeth Canori Mora, an Italian Trinitarian tertiary (1774–1825), whose writings received ecclesiastical approval, described chastisements in which "the sky was covered with clouds so dense that it was impossible to look at it." Fr. Alar records her prophecy that "demons will drag away the wicked while Saints Peter and Paul will gather up all good Catholics."

### Blessed Anna Maria Taigi (1769–1837)

Blessed Anna Maria Taigi, an Italian wife, mother, and mystic beatified in 1920, is one of the most frequently cited sources for the three days of darkness. Fr. Alar summarizes her core prophecies:

- "There would come over the earth a darkness lasting three days and three nights."
- "Nothing will be visible and the air will smell of death."
- During the darkness, **artificial light will not work** — "no flashlight" — only **blessed candles** can be lighted and they will give light.
- "Don't open the window. Don't look outside. Don't leave your house because you'll fall dead."

Fr. Alar cites the work of Rev. Gerald Culleton, whose book _Prophets and Our Times_ received both the _Nihil Obstat_ and _Imprimatur_, as providing an extensive study of Blessed Anna Maria Taigi's prophecies.

### Marie-Julie Jahenny (1850–1941)

The Breton mystic and stigmatist Marie-Julie Jahenny (the "Breton Stigmatist") is cited for the detail that "only blessed candles made of wax will give some light" and that "one candle will last for all three days." Additionally, "in the house of the God-fearing, the candles won't be put out," but "they will not give light in the houses of the godless."

Fr. Alar notes further tradition that demons may appear "disguised as family members coming to get us," but again emphasizes: "This is not dogma. This is just something that will help us prepare."

### Blessed Mary of Jesus Crucified (1846–1878)

The Discalced Carmelite mystic Blessed Mary of Jesus Crucified (Mariam Baouardy) is cited for the prophecy that "only a fourth of the humans will survive" the three days of darkness.

## 6. The Era of Peace and the Triumph of the Immaculate Heart

The culmination of the eschatological sequence according to the saints is not the Second Coming itself — which follows the era of peace — but the **Triumph of the Immaculate Heart of Mary**, prophesied at Fatima.

Fr. Alar places this in the following context:

> "Mary's Immaculate Heart will triumph at the end."

This triumph is:

- **Not** the Second Coming, but a **preparatory era** of peace granted to the world through the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary
- Connected to the **consecration of Russia** to the Immaculate Heart, as requested at Fatima
- Conditioned upon the Church's response — specifically, the practice of the **First Saturday devotion**, which Fr. Alar believes "we've not been doing"
- The era in which the Church will enjoy a period of peace, the Gospel will flourish, and the enemies of God will be defeated before the final Parousia

Fr. Alar references his own talk on the Immaculate Heart of Mary available on the Divine Mercy YouTube channel.

For the full magisterial and theological treatment of the Sacred Heart devotion, see [liturgy/sacred-heart-devotion.md](../liturgy/sacred-heart-devotion.md). For the Fatima apparition itself, see [miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/fatima.md](../miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/fatima.md).

## 7. Fatima and Akita: The Conditional Warnings

### Akita (1973, Japan)

Fr. Alar treats the apparition of Our Lady of Akita (1973, Japan), approved by Bishop John Shojiro Ito in 1984 and recognized as "worthy of belief" by the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, as a profoundly serious warning. The locutions given to Sister Agnes Sasagawa, a deaf Japanese convert, included:

> "If men do not repent, the Father will inflict a terrible punishment on all humanity. It will be a greater punishment than the flood, like nothing that's ever been seen."

The specific warnings include:

- **Fire falling from the sky**, wiping out "a great part of humanity — the good and the bad, nobody will be spared, priests even, faithful people"
- **Survivors will envy the dead**: "they'll find themselves very desolate, and in fact they'll envy the dead"
- **The only weapons**: "the rosary and the sign left by my Son Jesus"

Fr. Alar emphasizes that the Akita message, like Fatima, is **conditional** — it can be mitigated or averted through prayer, penance, and especially the daily recitation of the Rosary.

For a complete study of the Akita apparition, see [miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/akita.md](../miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/akita.md).

### Fatima (1917, Portugal)

The Fatima message is woven throughout the interview. Key points Fr. Alar highlights:

- **The three secrets of Fatima**: He invokes St. Thomas Aquinas's teaching that private revelation and prophecy "offers a deep understanding of revealed truths" and "gives guidance to the leaders of the Church for actions they should take at a particular point in time."
- **The 1884 vision of Pope Leo XIII**: Fr. Alar recounts the tradition that Pope Leo XIII overheard a conversation between Jesus and Satan, in which Satan asked for "more time and power" to bring down the Church. Christ granted this — to demonstrate that Satan cannot destroy the Church. This vision is connected by Fr. Alar to the composition of the St. Michael Prayer.
- **The 100-year hypothesis**: Fr. Alar reports two interpretations of when the "100 years" of Satan's increased power began — 1917 (coinciding with WWI, the rise of Marxist Russia, Margaret Sanger's birth control clinics, and the first Fatima apparition on May 13, which was also the Feast of Our Lady of the Eucharist and the day Pius XII was made a bishop) or 1984 (100 years after Pope Leo XIII's vision, the year St. John Paul II consecrated the world to the Immaculate Heart).
- **The First Saturdays**: Fr. Alar believes that while the consecration of Russia has been performed, the conversion of Russia has not yet fully occurred because "we've not been doing the First Saturdays."

For the complete Fatima study, see [miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/fatima.md](../miracles/marian-apparitions/approved/fatima.md).

## 8. Satan's Three Great Lies Today

When asked what Satan's biggest lies are, especially to the youth, Fr. Alar identifies "the same three non-negotiables that we Catholics cannot ever find alternatives to":

### 1. Abortion

The lie: overpopulation, women's rights, the child as "just a clump of tissue," convenience over life ("you've got your career to think about").

The truth: every human life is made in the image and likeness of God from the moment of conception. The direct killing of the innocent is intrinsically evil. See [sacraments/abortion-excommunication.md](../sacraments/abortion-excommunication.md) for the canonical penalty and the path of reconciliation.

### 2. The Redefinition of Marriage

The lie: marriage can exist between two men or two women.

The truth: marriage is the conjugal covenant between one man and one woman, ordered toward the procreation and education of children. "Even the sexuality of the man and woman are compatible. It's not compatible with two men. There's no place for that to bear children." See [sacraments/matrimony.md](../sacraments/matrimony.md) for the Catholic doctrine of marriage as a sacrament.

### 3. Transgenderism

The lie: your God-given sex is wrong; you are what you feel, not what God made you.

The truth: "We're made in the image and likeness of God. So what does Satan want us to do? Confuse what that image is. Tell mankind that your image is the wrong image." Fr. Alar calls this "preposterous, but very prevalent."

## 9. The Sacraments: The Only Remedy

The central pastoral thrust of Fr. Alar's message is sacrament-centered. When asked for his "one urgent message before the warning comes," he answers without hesitation:

> "Get the grace of the sacraments. There's only one way to heaven, and that is grace."

He unpacks this in several dimensions:

### State of Grace as the Fundamental Refuge

> "The biggest refuge is being in a state of grace."

When asked about the concept of physical "refuges" (a topic popularized by some traditionalist preachers), Fr. Alar responds carefully: "We have to be careful because these are not dogmatic." While he concedes that it "may be prudent to maybe have some supplies" for times of war or famine, he redirects the question to the sacramental life:

- "If we're in a state of grace, we're protected no matter what happens on the outside."
- "If you're not in a state of grace, nothing can help you — no medicine, no politician, no weapon, nothing."
- "It doesn't matter what happens around us. Jesus said that we have to stay inside the body of Christ. When we are in a state of grace, we're in the body of Christ. In that way, you don't have to fear. Nothing can harm you — no earthquake, no world war."

### The Danger of Treating Sacraments as Optional

> "A lot of people just treat the sacraments as something optional — something that we can have or not have, it doesn't matter. That's critically dangerous."

Fr. Alar insists that the sacraments are "absolutely necessary for salvation" because "you're not saved without grace, and God's grace He chose to give us through the sacraments." He promotes his book _Understanding the Sacraments_ (available on Amazon) as a catechetical tool.

### Anointing of the Sick and the Unconscious

In response to a viewer question about a mother with dementia who can no longer confess, Fr. Alar explains the pastoral provision for Anointing of the Sick:

- "Anointing gives the confession, the forgiveness of sins, even if a person is not consciously aware."
- The priest may anoint conditionally — "if they would want the grace" — when there is reason to believe the person was trying to live as a disciple of Christ.
- The soul still must accept the grace, and "God will give them the chance to do that."

### The Urgency of Confession and Confirmation

Fr. Alar specifically highlights two sacraments: **Confession** (the ordinary means for restoring the state of grace) and **Confirmation** (the sacramental strengthening for spiritual combat, which "some mystics have said you've got to make sure you're confirmed" for the tribulation).

## 10. Pope Leo XIV and the Age of AI

Fr. Alar offers a brief but illuminating commentary on Pope Leo XIV's pontificate:

- Pope Leo XIV took his name in conscious reference to Pope Leo XIII, whose encyclical _Rerum Novarum_ (1891) addressed the fear that machines would replace human labor during the Industrial Revolution.
- The present age faces a parallel anxiety: artificial intelligence threatening to replace human work and decision-making.
- Fr. Alar sees Leo XIV's election as providential: "Here comes Pope Leo XIV, and he's saying AI is a very scary thing to a lot of people. It's a very important thing that we have to understand."

He notes that "it's too early to tell" what the new pope's full role will be, but that the choice of name signals a continuity of concern for the dignity of human labor and the moral governance of technology.

## 11. Practical Exhortations

Throughout the interview, Fr. Alar returns to concrete, actionable steps for the Catholic faithful:

1. **Stay in a state of grace through the sacraments** — frequent Confession and worthy reception of the Holy Eucharist
2. **Pray the Rosary daily** — "the only weapons we will have are the Rosary and the sign left by my Son Jesus" (Our Lady of Akita)
3. **Practice the First Saturday devotion** — as requested at Fatima
4. **Turn to Divine Mercy** — "Divine Mercy is mankind's last hope of salvation. Faustina said mankind will not have peace until it turns with trust to His mercy."
5. **Learn your faith** — "You cannot love what you do not know. If you learn your faith, you can love your faith."
6. **Get confirmed** — for sacramental strengthening in spiritual warfare
7. **Consecrate yourself to Mary** — "God's giving us a choice to pick or to choose what we want."
8. **Prepare blessed beeswax candles** — for the possibility of the three days of darkness (as traditional prudence, not as a matter of faith)
9. **Watch the "Explaining the Faith" series** — Fr. Alar's catechetical talks on the Divine Mercy YouTube channel, including a three-part series on the end times

| **Divine Revelation / Dogma**        | The Second Coming of Christ (Parousia), the resurrection of the body, the Last Judgment, the reality of heaven and hell, the immortality of the soul      | De fide — binding on all Catholics                                 |
| **Scriptural Prophecy**              | The five biblical signs (preaching to all nations, the apostasy, the conversion of the Jews, the Antichrist, the tribulation)                             | Contained in Sacred Scripture; requires theological interpretation |
| **Catechism Teaching**               | CCC 670 (the "last hour"), CCC 674 (recognition by "all Israel"), CCC 675 (the Church's final trial), CCC 676–677 (the Antichrist)                        | Authoritative Magisterial teaching                                 |
| **Approved Private Revelation**      | Our Lady of Akita (approved by Bishop Ito, 1984), Our Lady of Fatima (approved by Bishop da Silva, 1930), St. Faustina's Diary (approved by the Holy See) | "Worthy of belief" but not binding on conscience (CCC 67)          |
| **Beatified Mystics (Non-Dogmatic)** | Blessed Anna Maria Taigi, Blessed Elizabeth Canori Mora, Blessed Mary of Jesus Crucified, Marie-Julie Jahenny                                             | Not defined teaching; pious belief permitted but not required      |
| **Theological Opinion**              | The "red dragon" as communist China; the "100-year" hypothesis regarding Pope Leo XIII's vision; the precise order of eschatological events               | Fr. Alar's personal theological synthesis; open to debate          |

Fr. Alar repeatedly and explicitly acknowledges these distinctions throughout the interview, stating: "The Church does not teach this is dogmatic or that you have to believe this. It's just something that I think is worth looking at."
