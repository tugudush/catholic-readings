# Catholic Miracles, Apparitions, Visions, and Prophecies

Welcome to the central directory documenting approved supernatural phenomena, wonders, and private revelations within the Catholic Church.

The Catholic Church maintains a posture of careful, scientifically rigorous, and pastoral discernment toward alleged supernatural occurrences, distinguishing clearly between the definitive public Revelation of Jesus Christ and private revelations.

---

## 1. Marian Apparitions

Marian apparitions are occurrences where the Blessed Virgin Mary is understood to have physically appeared to one or more seers with messages of prayer, repentance, and conversion. The Church classifies alleged apparitions by canonical status, and the documents below are organized into three categories: **approved** (declared "worthy of belief"), **ongoing investigations** (under study or not yet judged, e.g., the 2024 _nihil obstat_ granted to Medjugorje), and **rejected** (judged not of supernatural origin or formally prohibited).

### 1.1 Approved Apparitions

- **[Our Lady of the Pillar (c. 40 A.D.)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-the-pillar.md)**: The apostolic tradition of Mary's appearance to Saint James the Greater at Zaragoza, Spain — the oldest Marian shrine in the world and the Patroness of the Hispanic world.
- **[Our Lady of the Snows (c. 352–366)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-the-snows.md)**: The miraculous snowfall on the Esquiline Hill of Rome that founded the Liberian Basilica of Saint Mary Major.
- **[Our Lady of Guadalupe (1531)](marian-apparitions/approved/guadalupe.md)**: The appearance of Our Lady to Saint Juan Diego in Mexico in December 1531, which produced the miraculous tilma image and catalyzed the evangelization of the Americas.
- **[The Miraculous Medal (1830)](marian-apparitions/approved/miraculous-medal.md)**: The revelations to Saint Catherine Labouré in Paris, directing the striking of the famous medal bearing sacramental graces.
- **[Our Lady of La Salette (1846)](marian-apparitions/approved/la-salette.md)**: The sorrowful, weeping appearance of Mary to Mélanie Calvat and Maximin Giraud on a French mountain.
- **[Our Lady of Lourdes (1858)](marian-apparitions/approved/lourdes.md)**: The 18 apparitions of Mary as the "Immaculate Conception" to Saint Bernadette Soubirous in France, and the uncovering of the miraculous grotto spring.
- **[Our Lady of Good Help, Champion (1859)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-good-help-champion.md)**: The apparitions to Adèle Brise in Wisconsin — the first and only Church-approved apparition in the United States (approved 2010).
- **[Our Lady of Laus (1664–1718)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-laus.md)**: The long series of apparitions to Venerable Benoîte Rencurel in the French Alps, a shrine of the Sacrament of Reconciliation (approved 2008).
- **[Our Lady of Pontmain (1871)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-pontmain.md)**: The apparition of Our Lady of Hope to two French children during the Franco-Prussian War, with its written message of consolation (approved 1872).
- **[Our Lady of Knock (1879)](marian-apparitions/approved/knock.md)**: The silent, liturgical, Eucharistic apparition in Ireland, depicting Mary, Saint Joseph, Saint John the Evangelist, and the Lamb on the Altar.
- **[Our Lady of Aparecida (1717)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-aparecida.md)**: The miraculous recovery of the terracotta image from the Paraíba River — Patroness of Brazil and the most visited Marian shrine in the world.
- **[Our Lady of Fatima (1917)](marian-apparitions/approved/fatima.md)**: The apparitions to Francisco, Jacinta, and Lúcia in Portugal, featuring the Three Secrets and the Miracle of the Sun.
- **[Our Lady of Beauraing (1932–1933)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-beauraing.md)**: The "Golden Heart" apparitions to five Belgian children, with the promise of the conversion of sinners (approved 1949).
- **[Our Lady of Banneux (1933)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-banneux.md)**: The "Virgin of the Poor" apparitions to Mariette Beco in Belgium, with the healing spring "for all nations" (approved 1949).
- **[Our Lady of Akita (1973)](marian-apparitions/approved/akita.md)**: The modern apparitions and miraculous lacrimations of a wooden statue witnessed by Sister Agnes Sasagawa in Japan.
- **[Our Lady of Kibeho (1981–1989)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-kibeho.md)**: The first African approved apparition — the prophetic visions to Rwandan schoolgirls that foretold the 1994 genocide (approved 2001).
- **[Our Lady of San Nicolás (1983–1990)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-san-nicolas.md)**: The apparitions to Gladys Quiroga de Motta in Argentina, centered on the family and the Rosary (approved 2016).
- **[Our Lady of Zeitoun (1968–1971)](marian-apparitions/approved/our-lady-of-zeitoun.md)**: The luminous apparitions over a Coptic church in Cairo, recognized by the Coptic Orthodox Church and unopposed by the Catholic Church.

### 1.2 Ongoing Investigations

- **[Our Lady of Medjugorje (1981– )](marian-apparitions/ongoing-investigations/medjugorje.md)**: The most widely followed modern apparitions — granted a _nihil obstat_ by the 2024 Dicastery note _"The Queen of Peace"_ while its supernatural character remains undetermined.
- **[Our Lady of Garabandal (1961–1965)](marian-apparitions/ongoing-investigations/garabandal.md)**: The Spanish apparitions never approved by the Church (negative 1967 declaration) yet never formally condemned.
- **[Our Lady of America (1954–1960)](marian-apparitions/ongoing-investigations/our-lady-of-america.md)**: The unapproved Ohio revelations to Sister Mary Ephrem Neuzil, calling for a national shrine.
- **[Our Lady of the Revelation, Tre Fontane (1947)](marian-apparitions/ongoing-investigations/tre-fontane.md)**: The Roman vision to Bruno Cornacchiola — declared _non constat de supernaturalitate_ in 1948 yet with a tolerated cult and shrine.

### 1.3 Rejected Apparitions

- **[Our Lady of All Nations, Amsterdam (1945–1959)](marian-apparitions/rejected/amsterdam-lady-of-all-nations.md)**: Judged _constat de non supernaturalitate_ by the CDF (1974, approved by Pope Paul VI) and publicly confirmed by the Dicastery in 2024.
- **[Our Lady of Necedah (1950)](marian-apparitions/rejected/necedah.md)**: The Wisconsin apparitions to Mary Ann Van Hoof, formally condemned by the Diocese of La Crosse in 1955.
- **[Our Lady of Heroldsbach (1949)](marian-apparitions/rejected/heroldsbach.md)**: The Bavarian apparitions condemned by the Archbishop of Bamberg in 1952 and confirmed by the Holy Office in 1954.
- **[Our Lady of Marpingen (1876)](marian-apparitions/rejected/marpingen.md)**: The "German Lourdes" apparitions forbidden by the Bishop of Trier within months of their beginning.
- **[Our Lady of Palmar de Troya (1968– )](marian-apparitions/rejected/palmar-de-troya.md)**: The Spanish apparitions that produced the schismatic "Palmarian Catholic Church," whose leaders incurred excommunication.
- **[Bayside: "Our Lady of the Roses" (1970–1995)](marian-apparitions/rejected/bayside-our-lady-of-the-roses.md)**: The New York apparitions to Veronica Lueken, formally condemned by the Diocese of Brooklyn in 1986–1987.
- **[Our Lady of Conyers, Georgia (1987–1998)](marian-apparitions/rejected/conyers-georgia.md)**: The apparitions to Nancy Fowler, declared not of supernatural origin by the Archdiocese of Atlanta in 1997–1998.
- **[Our Lady of Emmitsburg (1993– )](marian-apparitions/rejected/emmitsburg-maryland.md)**: The Maryland apparitions to Gianna Talone Sullivan, rejected by the Archbishops of Baltimore in 1999 and 2010.
- **[Our Lady of Mount St. Patrick (1975– )](marian-apparitions/rejected/mount-st-patrick-cle-elum.md)**: The Washington State apparitions to "Mary Rose," rejected by the Diocese of Yakima in 1984.

## 2. Eucharistic Miracles

Eucharistic miracles are extraordinary physical signs that serve as motives of credibility (CCC 156) confirming the dogma of the Real Presence of Christ and the conversion of Transubstantiation, where the consecrated species visibly take on the attributes of human flesh and blood. They are private revelations — belief in any particular miracle is not required for salvation (CCC 67).

- **[List of Eucharistic Miracles](eucharistic-miracles/list-of-eucharistic-miracles.md)**: The comprehensive catalogue of more than 150 Church-credited Eucharistic miracles worldwide, organized by country following the Vatican-exhibited exhibition of Saint Carlo Acutis, with the modern scientifically studied cases (Buenos Aires, Tixtla, Sokółka, Legnica), the Church's discernment of rejected claims, and the theological principles governing these wonders.
- **[The Eucharistic Miracle of Bolsena-Orvieto (1263)](eucharistic-miracles/bolsena-orvieto.md)**: The bleeding Host that stained the corporal of Father Peter of Prague, prompting Pope Urban IV to institute the universal Feast of Corpus Christi.
- **[The Eucharistic Miracle of Lanciano (c. 750 AD)](eucharistic-miracles/lanciano.md)**: The earliest scientifically analyzed miracle, where the Host and wine became human cardiac muscle tissue (AB blood type) and remain perfectly preserved today.

## 3. Miracles and Wonders of the Saints

Phenomena and supernatural signs surrounding the earthly lives and relics of the canonized saints.

- **[The Stigmata of Saint Pio of Pietrelcina](saint-phenomena/stigmata-padre-pio.md)**: The physical configuration of Padre Pio to Christ's Passion, bearing his bleeding wounds for exactly fifty years until his death.
- **[The Liquefaction of the Blood of Saint Januarius](saint-phenomena/liquefaction-st-januarius.md)**: The century-spanning prodigy of Milan/Naples where the ancient, dried blood of the martyr liquefies on specific feast days.

## 4. Prophecies and Heavenly Visions

Mystical revelations, visions of Christ, and prophetic directions entrusted to saints to guide and renew the Church.

- **[Divine Mercy and Saint Faustina](prophecies-visions/divine-mercy-st-faustina.md)**: The revelations, image, chaplet, and feast of Mercy revealed to Saint Maria Faustina Kowalska in 1930s Poland.
- **[The Sacred Heart and Saint Margaret Mary Alacoque](prophecies-visions/sacred-heart-st-margaret-mary.md)**: The apparitions of Christ's burning Heart to a Visitation nun in Paray-le-Monial, establishing the Holy Hour, First Fridays devotion, and the Feast of the Sacred Heart. _For the comprehensive treatment of the Sacred Heart devotion as a whole — including its theological foundations, magisterial documents, liturgical solemnity, and prayers — see [liturgy/sacred-heart-devotion.md](../liturgy/sacred-heart-devotion.md)._

## Theological Principles of Discernment

1. **Definitive Public Revelation**: Catholic doctrine asserts that public Revelation ended with the death of the last Apostle. No private revelation can add to, subtract from, or correct this deposit of faith (CCC 66).
2. **Criteria for Approval**: The Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith (DDF) and bishops evaluate messages based on three pillars:
   - **Doctrinal Alignment**: The messages must contain nothing contrary to faith and morals.
   - **Seer Authenticity**: The seers must demonstrate psychological balance, humility, integrity, and obedience to the Church.
   - **Spiritual Fruits**: The event must lead to tangible spiritual growth, conversions, prayer, and charity.
3. **Levels of Assent**: Belief in approved apparitions is not a matter of dogmatic faith. Rather, the Church grants a _Nihil obstat_ or episcopal declaration stating the phenomenon is "worthy of belief," offering it to the faithful for their prudent spiritual benefit.
4. **The 2024 Norms and Categories of Judgment**: On 17 May 2024, the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith promulgated new _Norms for Proceeding in the Discernment of Alleged Supernatural Phenomena_, replacing the 1978 norms and introducing graduated outcomes: _nihil obstat_ (no obstacles — the faithful may prudently adhere, as with Medjugorje in 2024), _prae oculis habeatur_ (be kept in view), _curatur_ (pastoral care), _sub mandato_ (under mandate), and _prohibetur et obstruatur_ (prohibited). The three folders of [marian-apparitions/](marian-apparitions/) map onto this discernment: **approved** (formal declaration of authenticity or immemorial cult), **ongoing-investigations** (permitted or unjudged, without a declaration of supernatural origin), and **rejected** (judged not of supernatural origin or prohibited).
