# Our Lady of Aparecida (1717)

## Theological Introduction to Private Revelation

The devotion of Our Lady of Aparecida (Portuguese, _Nossa Senhora Aparecida_) is the greatest Marian devotion of Brazil and one of the most frequented shrines in the world. Strictly speaking, it does not rest upon an apparition in the classic sense but upon the **miraculous recovery of a small terracotta image** from the Paraíba River in 1717, accompanied by extraordinary events:

- The Church has ratified the devotion by centuries of episcopal and papal favor, and by the solemn proclamation of Our Lady of Aparecida as **Patroness of Brazil**.
- As a case of a miraculously recovered image and its providential protection, it belongs to the class of private revelation in the broad sense (CCC 67), subordinate to the public Revelation of Christ.

## The Recovery of the Image (1717)

In **October 1717**, three fishermen of the village of Guaratinguetá, in the captaincy of São Paulo — Domingos Garcia, João Alves, and Filipe Pedroso — were fishing in the Paraíba River in preparation for the visit of the Count of Assumar, the governor of the captaincy.

### Key Historical Details:

1. **The First Catch**: After a morning of fruitless fishing, João Alves cast his net and drew up the body of a small terracotta statue of the Blessed Virgin, broken at the neck and headless, dark in color from the river's mud. He cast the net again and brought up the head of the same statue, which fitted perfectly onto the body.
2. **The Abundance of Fish**: Immediately afterward, the nets filled with fish so abundantly that the boat was nearly swamped — an event the fishermen and the people attributed to the intercession of the recovered image, and which is commemorated as the first sign of the devotion.
3. **The Veneration**: The image was taken to the home of Filipe Pedroso and venerated by the neighborhood; a chapel was soon built at a place called Itaguaçu. Miracles and answered prayers multiplied, and the devotion spread throughout Brazil.
4. **The Darkened Image**: The image is notable for its dark color, which gives the title _Aparecida_ ("appeared" or "found"); it is a small statue of the Immaculate Conception in terracotta, about 36 cm in height, and has been repeatedly restored and honored.
5. **The Crown and the Pontifical Acts**: In 1904 the image was solemnly crowned in the name of Pope Saint Pius X; in 1930, Pope Pius XI solemnly proclaimed **Our Lady of Aparecida the Patroness of Brazil**, and in 1980 Pope Saint John Paul II consecrated the great National Basilica and celebrated Mass there before hundreds of thousands.

## Ecclesiastical Authority

- **Patronage (1930)**: The decree of Pope Pius XI (1929, promulgated 1930) declared Our Lady of Aparecida the principal Patroness of Brazil before God, with a proper feast celebrated on **October 12**, now a national holiday in Brazil.
- **The National Basilica**: The Basilica of the National Shrine of Our Lady of Aparecida, consecrated by Saint John Paul II in 1980, is one of the largest churches in the world and receives millions of pilgrims annually, making Aparecida the most visited Marian shrine on earth.
- **Papal Visits**: Successive popes — Saint John Paul II (1980 and 1997), Pope Benedict XVI (2007, on the occasion of the Fifth General Conference of the Latin American Bishops' Council, CELAM, whose concluding document bears the name "Aparecida"), and Pope Francis (2013) — have visited the shrine, sealing the devotion's place at the heart of Latin American Catholicism.

## The Message and Devotion

The devotion of Aparecida proclaims the **maternal protection of the Immaculate Conception over the Brazilian people**: the recovery of the image and the abundant catch are understood as signs of Mary's care for the humble and poor, and the shrine is a center of popular piety, pilgrimages, and evangelization. The "Aparecida Document" of 2007 made the shrine emblematic of the Church's missionary renewal in Latin America: "disciples and missionaries of Jesus Christ, so that our peoples may have life in Him."
