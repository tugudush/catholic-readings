# Our Lady of Beauraing (1932–1933)

## Theological Introduction to Private Revelation

The apparitions of Our Lady of Beauraing, in the French-speaking province of Namur, Belgium, are among the most fully investigated and formally approved apparitions of the twentieth century:

- As private revelation (CCC 67), the events are subordinate to the public Revelation of Christ but were judged "worthy of belief" by the local Ordinary.
- Beauraing, with the contemporary apparitions of Banneux, belongs to the great Belgian series of 1932–1933, whose message of prayer and conversion for the whole world was delivered to children.

## The Apparitions to the Five Children

Between **November 29, 1932** and **January 3, 1933**, the Blessed Virgin Mary appeared thirty-three times to five children of Beauraing: **Fernande Voisin (15)**, **Gilberte Voisin (13)**, **Albert Voisin (11)**, **Andrée Degeimbre (14)**, and **Gillette Degeimbre (9)**. The apparitions occurred near the grotto and the hawthorn hedge of the local convent school, where the children had gone to await a companion.

### Key Historical Details:

1. **The Appearances**: Mary appeared clothed in white, with a crown of gold, a blue veil, and a rosary over her arm. Her heart, encircled by rays of gold, was visible on her breast — the "Golden Heart" of Beauraing.
2. **The Message**: The Queen of Heaven called the children to **prayer and penance**, asking for the building of a chapel and urging that people come in pilgrimage. She declared, "I am the Immaculate Virgin," "Pray always," and "I will convert sinners."
3. **The Promise**: On the final apparition of January 3, 1933, Mary promised: "I will convert sinners." The children reported that she blessed them and exhorted them to pray.
4. **The Witnesses**: The apparitions drew enormous crowds — sometimes tens of thousands — and were accompanied by reported healings and conversions. The children were subjected to lengthy and repeated interrogation by civil and ecclesiastical authorities, including being separated and examined independently; their accounts remained consistent.
5. **The Golden Heart**: The distinctive sign of the Beauraing apparitions is the radiant heart of gold on Mary's breast, which the seers described as the source of the greatest light, and which gave the devotion its characteristic title.

## Ecclesiastical Approval

- **Canonical Approval (1949)**: On **July 2, 1949**, Bishop André-Marie Charue of Namur declared the apparitions of Beauraing authentic and authorized public devotion, concluding that the events bore the marks of the supernatural.
- **The Basilica**: A basilica was erected at the site of the apparitions, consecrated in 1954, and Beauraing became one of the principal Marian shrines of Belgium and of Europe.
- **Papal Recognition**: The devotion received repeated papal encouragement, including pilgrimages and indulgences granted by the Holy See, and the shrine remains under the pastoral care of the diocese.

## The Message and Devotion

The message of Beauraing is a call to **prayer, penance, and confidence in the conversion of sinners**, summarized in Mary's title as the "Immaculate Virgin" with the "Golden Heart." The devotion invites the faithful to trust in her intercession for the conversion of the world and to respond to her summons to a life of prayer and reparation.
