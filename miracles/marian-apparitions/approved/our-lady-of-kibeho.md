# Our Lady of Kibeho (1981–1989)

## Theological Introduction to Private Revelation
The apparitions of Our Lady of Kibeho, in the Diocese of Gikongoro, Rwanda, are the first formally approved Marian apparitions of Africa:
- As private revelation (CCC 67), the events are subordinate to the public Revelation of Christ but were judged authentic by the competent ecclesiastical authority in 2001.
- Kibeho is historically significant for its prophetic character: the messages foretold the coming Rwandan Genocide of 1994 and issued a call to conversion, prayer, and penance that took on its full gravity only after the tragedy.

## The Apparitions to the Schoolgirls
Between **November 28, 1981** and **November 28, 1989**, the Blessed Virgin Mary appeared to a number of students of the Kibeho College, a secondary school of the Diocese of Gikongoro. The principal seers were **Alphonsine Mumureke (16)**, **Nathalie Mukamazimpaka (17)**, and **Marie-Claire Mukangango (21)**.

### Key Historical Details:
1. **The First Apparitions**: On November 28, 1981, while the students were at recreation, Alphonsine Mumureke heard a voice calling her and saw a beautiful Lady of extraordinary radiance, who identified herself as the "**Mother of the Word**" (*Nyina wa Jambo*). The Lady asked for prayer, penance, and conversion.
2. **The Other Seers**: Nathalie Mukamazimpaka and then Marie-Claire Mukangango began to receive apparitions; Marie-Claire's experiences were initially marked by violent phenomena attributed to diabolical interference before the apparitions of the Virgin prevailed.
3. **The Messages**: The core message was a call to **conversion, prayer, and penance**, with special insistence on the Rosary. Mary is said to have wept over the sins of the world and to have called Rwanda to reconciliation.
4. **The Vision of the Genocide**: Beginning in 1982, the seers reported visions of rivers of blood, mutilated bodies, and the destruction of Rwanda — understood after 1994 as a prophetic warning of the genocide. Marie-Claire Mukangango reported a vision of the future massacres and perished in the genocide herself in 1994, along with many of the faithful of the region; Nathalie Mukamazimpaka and others survived.
5. **The Witnesses**: The apparitions drew thousands of pilgrims, including public phenomena and reported healings and conversions; the school and shrine became a place of intense prayer, especially of the Rosary.

## Ecclesiastical Approval
- **The 2001 Decree**: On **June 29, 2001**, Bishop **Augustin Misago** of Gikongoro, after a lengthy canonical investigation, declared the apparitions to **Alphonsine Mumureke** authentic and the essential core of the Kibeho message "worthy of belief," while prudently reserving judgment on the more particular messages of the other seers. This made Kibeho the first Church-approved apparition on the African continent.
- **The Sanctuary**: The shrine of Our Lady of Kibeho was erected at the site, and a memorial to the victims of the genocide was established at the college, integrating the message of conversion with the call to national reconciliation.
- **Episcopal Reception**: The approval was received by the Bishops of Rwanda and has been the object of continued pastoral care, with pilgrimages and the ministry of reconciliation at the sanctuary.

## The Message and Devotion
The message of Kibeho is a prophetic call to **conversion, prayer, and penance**, and — in light of 1994 — to **reconciliation and the healing of memory**. The "Mother of the Word" summoned Rwanda and the world to turn from sin before the catastrophe, and after the genocide the shrine has become a place of mourning, healing, and hope. The devotion emphasizes the Rosary as the school of prayer and peace.
