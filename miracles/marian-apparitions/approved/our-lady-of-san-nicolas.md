# Our Lady of the Rosary of San Nicolás (1983–1990)

## Theological Introduction to Private Revelation
The apparitions of Our Lady of the Rosary of San Nicolás, in the Province of Buenos Aires, Argentina, are among the most recent apparitions to receive formal episcopal approval:
- As private revelation (CCC 67), the events are subordinate to the public Revelation of Christ but were judged authentic by the local Ordinary in 2016.
- The devotion is notable for its strong emphasis on the **family, the Rosary, and the sanctification of daily life**, and for its center in a modern industrial city rather than a rural or mountainous setting.

## The Apparitions to Gladys Quiroga de Motta
Between **September 25, 1983** and **February 11, 1990**, the Blessed Virgin Mary appeared to **Gladys Quiroga de Motta**, a married laywoman and mother of two, of San Nicolás de los Arroyos, Argentina. The visions occurred principally in the family home, in the parish church of St. Nicholas, and at the site of the future shrine.

### Key Historical Details:
1. **The First Apparitions**: In September 1983, Gladys, a devout but unremarkable housewife, began to perceive the presence of the Blessed Virgin and to receive interior messages calling her to prayer and to the Rosary. The Virgin asked that a statue be made in her honor under the title "Our Lady of the Rosary of San Nicolás."
2. **The Medallion of 1983**: On November 21, 1983, an image of the Virgin appeared on a small metal medallion belonging to the family — an event that drew public attention and was the first of several extraordinary signs associated with the devotion.
3. **The Messages**: The messages, later collected in volumes, emphasize **prayer, the family, the Rosary, conversion, and trust in God**, together with a call to reparation and to fidelity in the ordinary duties of life. The Virgin is said to have asked for the building of a shrine and for the diffusion of devotion to the Rosary of San Nicolás.
4. **The Investigation and Prudence**: The local Church proceeded with great caution. Bishop Domingo Salvador Castagna, who first examined the events, remained prudent; the cause was pursued over decades with medical, psychological, and theological examination of the seer, who submitted throughout to ecclesiastical authority.
5. **The Shrine**: A shrine church was eventually erected at the site indicated in the messages, and the devotion spread widely in Argentina and beyond.

## Ecclesiastical Approval
- **The 2016 Decree**: On **May 22, 2016**, Bishop **Héctor Sabatino Cardelli** of San Nicolás de los Arroyos issued a decree declaring the apparitions of Our Lady of the Rosary of San Nicolás **authentic and of supernatural origin**, and approving the devotion and the title. The decree concluded a canonical process begun in the 1980s and is among the most recent formal approvals of a Marian apparition by a diocesan bishop.
- **The Shrine and Pilgrimage**: The sanctuary of San Nicolás has become a major pilgrimage center of Argentina, and the devotion to the "Rosary of San Nicolás" — with its own prayer and medal — has spread to other countries.

## The Message and Devotion
The message of San Nicolás is a call to **prayer, the family, and fidelity in ordinary life**, centered on the Rosary and on trust in the maternal intercession of the Blessed Virgin. The devotion invites families to consecrate themselves to Mary and to live the Gospel in the midst of the world, and the 2016 approval has encouraged its diffusion while the shrine continues its pastoral mission.
