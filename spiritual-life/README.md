# Spiritual Life

The [spiritual-life/](./) directory presents Catholic teaching and spiritual theology concerning sanctification, the interior life, prayer, virtue, ascetical purification, contemplation, and the perfection of charity. Documents distinguish binding doctrine from theological frameworks, devotional practice, and pastoral counsel.

- **Christian Spiritual Perfection and the Interior Life**: Read [sanctified-perfection-and-holiness.md](sanctified-perfection-and-holiness.md) for a structured synthesis of Fr. Chad Ripperger's lecture on sanctified perfection, grace, acquired and infused virtue, the three ways of the interior life, prayer, distraction, the sacraments, and disinterested charity.

The directory complements the repository's dedicated treatments of the [seven sacraments](../sacraments/README.md), [foundational Catholic prayers](../liturgy/foundational-prayers.md), [saints](../saints/README.md), and [fasting and abstinence](../liturgy/fasting-abstinence.md).
