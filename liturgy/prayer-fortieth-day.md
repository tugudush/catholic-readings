# Prayer for the Fortieth Day after Death

The **fortieth day** after death is one of the most significant commemorations in Catholic tradition. It recalls:

- Christ's **Ascension**, forty days after the Resurrection (Acts 1:3)
- Moses' **forty days on Mount Sinai** (Exodus 24:18)
- The Israelites' **forty years in the desert** (Numbers 14:33)
- Christ's **forty days of fasting** in the wilderness (Matthew 4:2)

The fortieth day marks a special transition — the soul is believed to have completed its initial purification and to be in a state of rest, awaiting the final judgment. It is a fitting time to offer prayers, especially the Holy Mass, for the repose of the deceased.

> _"Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May their souls, and the souls of all the faithful departed, through the mercy of God, rest in peace. Amen."_

---

## How to Use This Prayer

This prayer may be prayed privately at home, at the graveside, or before the Blessed Sacrament. Replace **N.** with the name of your beloved departed. Where feminine pronouns appear, substitute masculine as needed.

> **The most powerful gift** you can offer for the deceased on this day is the **Holy Mass**. Contact your parish to request a Mass intention for the repose of the soul of N.

---

## Prayer for the Fortieth Day

    In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

---

    O God, who commanded us to honor our father and mother,
    look with mercy upon the soul of Your servant N.,
    whom You have called from this life to Yourself.
    On this fortieth day,
    as the Church recalls the Ascension of Your Son
    and the fulfillment of His promise of eternal life,
    I commend N. into Your loving hands.
    Cleanse N. of every stain of sin,
    and grant entrance into Your heavenly kingdom,
    where there is no sorrow, no suffering, but only peace and joy
    in the light of Your countenance.
    Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

---

### The _Requiem Aeternam_ (Eternal Rest)

    Eternal rest grant unto N., O Lord,
    and let perpetual light shine upon N.
    May N. rest in peace. Amen.

### The _De Profundis_ (Psalm 129/130)

    Out of the depths I cry to You, O Lord;
    Lord, hear my voice.
    Let Your ears be attentive
    to my voice in supplication.
    If You, O Lord, mark iniquities,
    Lord, who can stand?
    But with You is forgiveness,
    that You may be revered.
    I wait for the Lord, my soul waits,
    and I hope in His word.
    My soul waits for the Lord
    more than sentinels wait for the dawn;
    more than sentinels wait for the dawn,
    let Israel hope for the Lord,
    for with the Lord is kindness
    and with Him is plenteous redemption,
    and He will redeem Israel
    from all their iniquities.

    Eternal rest grant unto N., O Lord,
    and let perpetual light shine upon N.
    May N., and all the souls of the faithful departed,
    through the mercy of God, rest in peace. Amen.

---

### Prayer of Commendation

    Remember, O Lord, Your servant N.,
    who has gone before us with the sign of faith
    and rests in the sleep of peace.
    Grant N., O Lord, we beseech You,
    a place of refreshment, light, and peace,
    that N. may pass from death
    to that life which never ends,
    through Christ our Lord. Amen.

---

### The _In Paradisum_

    May the angels lead N. into paradise;
    may the martyrs come to welcome N. on the way,
    and lead N. into the holy city, Jerusalem.
    May the choir of angels welcome N.,
    and with Lazarus, who once was poor,
    may N. have eternal rest. Amen.

---

### Concluding Prayer

    O Immaculate Virgin Mary, Mother of God and Mother of mercy,
    intercede for the soul of N.
    St. Joseph, patron of a happy death, pray for N.
    All you holy angels and saints,
    welcome N. into the heavenly Jerusalem.

    Eternal rest grant unto N., O Lord,
    and let perpetual light shine upon N.
    May N. rest in peace. Amen.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

## Additional Practices for the Fortieth Day

| Practice                         | Description                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Requiem Mass**                 | The most powerful prayer — have a Mass offered for the repose of the soul of N.              |
| **Rosary (Glorious Mysteries)**  | The Resurrection, Ascension, Descent of the Holy Spirit, Assumption, and Coronation of Mary. |
| **Visiting the Cemetery**        | Visit N.'s grave and pray the _Requiem Aeternam_. A partial indulgence may be gained.        |
| **Act of Charity or Almsgiving** | Offer a work of mercy or a donation in N.'s name, uniting it with prayers for N.'s soul.     |
| **Office of the Dead**           | The Liturgy of the Hours includes a full Office of the Dead that may be prayed on this day.  |
