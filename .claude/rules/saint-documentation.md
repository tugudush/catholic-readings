# Catholic Saint Documentation

- For a new biography or substantial expansion, use the [saint-documentation skill](../skills/saint-documentation/SKILL.md). For a focused revision, preserve existing content and apply the relevant safeguards without rewriting the article.
- State the subject's correct ecclesial status and qualify uncertain dates, authorship, relics, miracles, and traditions. Distinguish contemporary evidence, later hagiography, ecclesial judgments, private revelation, and theological opinion.
- Cite the saint's works, contemporary witnesses, official ecclesial sources, and the Magisterium precisely where applicable. Do not invent quotations or present disputed attributions as established.
- Separate prayers written by or securely attributed to the saint from prayers asking for the saint's intercession. Identify newly composed prayers as such and follow the [prayer formatting rule](prayer-formatting.md).
- Describe Latin and relevant Eastern Catholic observances when they materially differ. Use relative links and preserve the repository's doctrinal and writing conventions.
